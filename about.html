Who We Are

About InfiSource Global

InfiSource Global is a one-stop procurement and strategic sourcing partner to corporates, industries, institutions and commercial establishments across India. We manage recurring and project-based purchasing through a single, accountable point of contact — giving purchase teams one relationship to manage instead of dozens of vendors to coordinate.

  • Multi-Category Sourcing — office, industrial and institutional requirements consolidated under one roof.
  • Strategic Vendor Management — structured, performance-managed supplier relationships built for consistency.
  • PAN-India Supplier Network — a verified network of manufacturers and distributors spanning the country.
  • Quality, Cost & Delivery — every order benchmarked against all three, on every purchase.

Our sourcing team works to your specification and preferred brand, drawing on a verified, multi-brand supplier network rather than a fixed product list — giving purchase teams the reach of a large sourcing organisation with the accountability of a single partner.
